Who it's for

For small businesses that want practical bookkeeping support.

This service is designed for entrepreneurs who need help keeping basic business records organised, tracking expenses or understanding what information needs to be prepared for accounting.

It is particularly suitable for small businesses and sole traders with relatively straightforward bookkeeping needs.

What's included

  • Basic bookkeeping support
  • Expense tracking and organisation
  • Receipt and transaction organisation
  • Basic VAT-related record support
  • Preparation of information for accounting
  • Practical explanations of your records

How it works

Keep your records organised.

1

Review your current records

We look at how you currently handle receipts, transactions, expenses and other business records.

2

Set up a simple workflow

3

4

Sample scenario

“My business transactions are becoming difficult to track.”

You are a new sole trader with business purchases, invoices and receipts coming from several places. KF Biz can help you organise the records, establish a simple routine and identify what information should be kept for your accounting and tax obligations.
