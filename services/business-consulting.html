Business Consulting

Practical support for entrepreneurs and small businesses that need clearer plans, better information and confident next steps.

Who it's for

For entrepreneurs who need a clearer direction.

This service is suitable if you are planning a business, recently started one, or are facing a specific business decision and want structured, practical input.

It can be especially useful for business planning, startup grant or loan preparation, market analysis, pricing questions and general business decision-making.

What's included

  • Business idea and plan review
  • Business plan support
  • Basic market and competitor analysis
  • Startup cost and financial planning support
  • Pricing and profitability discussions
  • Practical decision support

How it works

A simple process.

1

Understand your situation

We discuss your business, goals, current stage and the question you need to solve.

2

Structure the problem

3

Work through the options

Leave with a clear next step

Sample scenario

“I want to open a small restaurant in Turku.”

You have the business idea but need to understand startup costs, competitors, pricing and whether the numbers make sense. KF Biz can help structure the business plan, review the basic financial assumptions and identify the next actions before you invest heavily.
