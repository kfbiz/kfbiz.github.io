Other Business Advisory

Flexible business-related guidance for entrepreneurs who need practical help with a specific question, task or administrative challenge.

From €35/hour + VAT

Who it's for

For business owners with a specific problem to solve.

This flexible service is for situations that do not fit neatly into consulting or bookkeeping but still need practical business support.

Examples include business administration, Finnish business paperwork, documentation, forms, basic financial questions and other day-to-day business tasks.

What's included

  • Business administration support
  • Forms and documentation assistance
  • Basic financial guidance
  • Business-related paperwork support
  • Research and information gathering
  • Other agreed practical tasks

How it works

Flexible support, focused on the task.

1

Tell us what you need

Explain the task, question or business problem and provide any relevant documents or background.

2

Confirm the scope

We clarify what can be handled, what information is needed and the expected amount of work.

3

Work on the task

KF Biz handles the agreed support and keeps the work practical and focused.

4

Deliver and explain

You receive the result and, where useful, a clear explanation of the next step.

Sample scenario

“I have a Finnish business form I don't know how to handle.”

You have a business-related form or administrative task and are unsure what information is needed or how to organise it. KF Biz can help you understand the task, prepare the information and complete the agreed practical support.
