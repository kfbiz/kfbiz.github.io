About KF Biz

Practical guidance for building a business in Finland.

KF Biz helps entrepreneurs make sense of the steps, numbers and decisions involved in starting and developing a business.

Meet the founder

Kyeonghwan Kim

Founder of KF Biz · Turku, Finland

KF Biz was founded by Kyeonghwan Kim, a business graduate with a background in finance, customer service, and investment analysis. After gaining experience in both South Korea and Finland, he brings international insight and a practical, client-first approach to every service.

His goal is to make business information easier to understand and help entrepreneurs take informed, realistic next steps in the Finnish business environment.

Our approach

Support built around your next step.

Who this is for

KF Biz is for people planning to start a business, entrepreneurs who have recently launched, and small business owners in Finland who want practical guidance with business planning, financial basics, paperwork or day-to-day administration. We aim to make the process more approachable, including for people navigating the Finnish business environment for the first time.

How I work

I start by understanding your situation, goals and immediate challenges. From there, I provide clear explanations, structured guidance and realistic, practical options so you can make informed decisions. The focus is on honest communication, useful next steps and support tailored to your needs.
